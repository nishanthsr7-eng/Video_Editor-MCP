## AI & Automation

Tools that use AI to automate editing tasks like captions, silence removal, and footage search.

- **AutoCut** — Automates captions, zooms, B-roll matching, silence/repetition removal. Good for podcasts. (Paid)
- **BadWords** — Local text-based editor inside Resolve. Transcribes audio, displays as text for editing; edits map back to the timeline. Can compare recording against a written script. Free, offline. (Free)
- **FireCut** — Silence removal, chapter markers, zoom cuts. Has a free tier. (Free tier + Paid)
- **Jumper** — AI footage search — find a specific visual moment or spoken word across your project. (Paid)
- **StoryToolkit AI** — Transcripts and translations powered by AI, similar to Avid's ScriptSync. (Paid)
- **Tagger for Resolve** — AI keyword tagging that makes footage searchable via smart bins. macOS. (Paid)

## Audio Plugins

VST/AU plugins for mixing, mastering, noise reduction, and effects.

- **Auburn Sounds** — Gates, dynamics, voice modulation, spatialization. Offers free versions.
- **iZotope RX** — Industry standard for dialogue noise reduction and audio cleanup.
- **kiloHearts** — EQ, Gain, Compressor and more with a clean UI. Essential plugins are free.
- **TBProAudio** — EQs, meters, and limiters.
- **Valhalla DSP** — Reverb plugins; several free options.
- **Waves** — EQs, compressors, cleanup, reverbs, meters.
- **ViatorDSP** — Lo-fi and EQ plugins, many free.

## DCTLs

DaVinci Color Transform Language plugins for color science and creative grading.

- **Baldavenger DCTLs** — Color science DCTLs, open source.
- **Thatcher Freeman** — Utility DCTLs. Highly regarded.
- **Mononodes** — Film emulations, hue twist, utility DCTLs. (Paid)
- **Nx Color** — ChromaDrift, Liquid Glass, CanvasFlow, ShadowCast. (Paid)
- **PixelTools** — DCTL plugins and PowerGrade presets with demo versions. (Paid)
- **Kaur Hendrikson** — Well-regarded DCTL pack with trial versions. (Paid)
- **Iridescent Color** — DCTL provider. (Paid)
- **Ravengrade** — DCTL provider. (Paid)
- **Salamifish** — DCTL provider with demo versions. (Paid)
- **Colorist Foundry Filmverse** — DCTL provider. (Paid)

## OFX / Video Effects

OpenFX plugins for visual effects, grain, film emulation, and diffusion.

- **Boris FX Continuum / Sapphire** — Industry standard VFX, color, and effects suite. (Paid)
- **CineMatch** — Camera matching plugin by FilmConvert. (Paid)
- **Colourlab.ai** — AI grading, look design, film grain. (Paid)
- **FilmConvert Nitrate** — Film emulation with grain. (Paid)
- **Neat Video** — Best-in-class video noise reduction. (Paid)
- **RE:Vision Effects** — Optical flow retiming, morph, and more. (Paid)
- **Dehancer** — Film emulation. (Paid)
- **CorridorKey-Runtime** — AI keying OFX plugin for Resolve. Free, Windows (RTX) + macOS (Apple Silicon). (Free)
- **Akascape** — Pixel sorting, Datamosh, VHS, Glitch, Rutt-Etra effects. (Paid)
- **Blewtoof** — CRT Machine, Mixed Media, Camcorder, Superchrome. (Paid)

## Workflow & Integration

Tools that extend or connect to Resolve's workflow.

- **DaVinci Resolve Speed Editor** — Dedicated hardware editing keyboard with built-in trackball. (Hardware)
- **PostLab** — Collaborative project management and version control for Resolve. (Paid)
- **Sync-N-Link** — Multi-camera audio sync and clip naming tool. (Paid)
- **CommandPost** — macOS utility for custom keyboard shortcuts, MIDI control, and automation. (Free)
- **MixPre Series (Sound Devices)** — Field recorders with Resolve metadata integration. (Hardware)
